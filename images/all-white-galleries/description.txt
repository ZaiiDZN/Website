All White Galleries is my second creative collection, funded by the Brown University Art Fellowship Grant. Six multi-media pieces, all created in one summer.

When I first thought about making a new project, I wanted to create something extremely detailed, that showcased and proved my technical abilities. Eventually I realized that I was creating work for the validation of people I wanted to acknowledge me, not for myself and those who I wanted to feel seen. All White Galleries is a project fueled by the goal of self- actualisation and discovery. Looking at the finished project, I would say that A.W.G. was not what I originally envisioned. I am definitely not satisfied with certain things. However, I've learned that I don't create art to be perfect. This project has become the home of some of my "quiet favorite" pieces. Their place in my art journey transformed from ideas that would be approved by a some unknown viewer, to pieces that I felt comfortable pouring myself into.
