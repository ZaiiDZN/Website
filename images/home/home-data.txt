about me:
Born in Brooklyn, NY, I am a ceramicist who explores emotion and statement through clay. At first, ceramics was a safe space for me, and I spent many hours during and after school working in the studio. It pushed me to confront myself and learn to be patient with myself on the roller-coaster of life. After taking a studio art class in high school, I realized that I wanted my pieces to be a reflection of myself and what I saw through my life experiences. My work embraces imperfection, as our shortcomings are also part of our story. I aim to make pieces that feel alive, raw, and otherworldly. I also study Architectural Design at Brown University, where I have learned key design principles that help me understand the discipline, as well as improve my own creative process. Today, my sculptures are both intentional and interpretive. They are a mirror for people to see themselves, as well as a looking glass to see the world through my eyes. I hope to continue to create these representations of life, not just with ceramics, but other mediums.
